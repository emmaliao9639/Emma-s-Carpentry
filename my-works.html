My Works

The DIM Workshop I apprentice provides classes of different levels, suitable for kids of different ages and carpentry experiences. Advancing through classes, apprentices practice their skills while learning new tools, machines, and various crafting skills.

Through my years in the workshop, I have created dozens of works. Check out my works at different stages!

Rudiment

Stage 01

This is where everything started. In this stage, every class began with a picture book, from which we recreate a character, a prop, or a scene. We use simple tools like bow saw, hand drill, white glue, sand paper, and acrylic paint for coloring.

Structure

Stage 02

Moving on from simple glueing, apprentices at this stage learned to create movable structures by applying fit tolerance to round poles as well as using other materials such as strings and springs. During this process, the teacher began to introduce machine tools, allowing the apprentice to create more delicate works. This is the stage where apprentices lay a solid foundation of crafting skills.

Mechanism

Stage 03

Suitable for slightly older kids, this class incorporates mechanism into carpentry, including gears, pulleys, levers, etc. Starting each class, the teacher would first explain the principle behind the mechanism used that day, allowing the apprentice to understand and thus apply that knowledge in future works.

Lathe / Lazer Cutting

Stage 04

The lathe class and the lazer cutting class are both optional classes provided for apprentices who had advanced to the Creation Class (where the apprentice can decide topics on their own). Learning these advanced skills can greatly extend the possibility of one's creation: lathing can make various solids of revolution quickly and with smooth polishing, while lazer cutting can engrave various shapes and patterns onto the wood's surface, as well as cutting small, delicate pieces that are challenging to create using hand or machine tools.

Weapons / Props / Other

Stage 05

In the Creation Class, I can decide topics on my own. This class is where I realize my dreams. My favorite topic is weapons: I love to plane and polish the curve of the blade, as well as add details on the handle. Moreover, I replicate weapons and props from Marvel movies, the Jing-Yong series, and other films/anime I like. While each work takes a longer amount of time to complete, they are by far my favorites.
