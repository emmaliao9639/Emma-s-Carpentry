Processing Skills

1. Safety

Safety. Safety. Safety. This is the most important skill to learn — how to ensure the safety of oneself and others. Some essential rules include:

  • No sandals or slippers. One must wear shoes that cover the entire foot and all toes.
  • Tie one's hair if it's too long, to avoid it being entangled by the machinery.
  • When cutting, drilling, or sanding smaller pieces, do NOT grab them with bare hands. Use other tools such as a vise to hold the pieces.
  • Keep body parts away from the blade, drill, or the disk of the sanding machine.
  • After cutting/drilling, ALWAYS put down the tool/shut down the machine before grabbing the wood piece.
  • Wear a mask/goggles when using tools or machines that may splatter sawdust (ex: lathe machine, sanding machine).
Safety. Safety. Safety.
